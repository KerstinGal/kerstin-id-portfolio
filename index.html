How I work

From brief to behaviour change

A structured approach that doesn't disappear under deadline pressure. Each phase has a clear deliverable — so stakeholders know where they are, and I know what I'm building.

01
Analyse

Stakeholder interviews, audience analysis, performance gap diagnosis. What actually needs to change — and why?

02
Design

Learning objectives, structure, format decisions. Outline Concept signed off before a single slide is built.

03
Develop

Storyboard → prototype → full build in Storyline 360, Rise, or blended format. Stakeholder review in Articulate Review.

04
Implement

SCORM/xAPI packaging, LMS upload and testing, learner communications. I handle the handover — not just the handoff.

05
Evaluate

Kirkpatrick levels 1–3 where feasible. Completion rates, knowledge check data, and qualitative feedback to close the loop.
